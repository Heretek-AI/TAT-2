  decal record (none in the extraction).

  `/Game/Art/Environment/Props/Containers/Barrels/SM_Metal_BarrelA/B/C` exists
  and is placed elsewhere in PeripheryA (60 `SM_Metal_Barrel*` instances map-wide

  alley (`[-9,-5] x [68,79]`). No placement was invented.
