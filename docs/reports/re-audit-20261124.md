# RE-audit — how faithful is the port of the shipped UE5.5 build?

**Date:** 2026-11-25 · **Audited tree:** lanes at `38e7b03`, gates at `6760dd0` ·
**Inputs:** 17 RE lane reports, 5 C lanes, 5 L lanes, `F1a`/`F1b` adjudications,
`REMEDIATION_PLAN.md`.

**Scope.** This is a synthesis; it adds no measurement of its own. Every number is
traceable to a lane finding (`RE-x-nn`), an adjudication, or a `file:line` the lane
quoted and this report re-read. A lane number that failed re-check is carried as failed.
Where two lanes still disagree, the settling experiment is named.

---

## 1. Executive summary

1. **The extraction is broader than the documentation claims.** 107/107 shipped maps
   normalise (`RE-O`, `RE-B`), 995/995 animation clips convert (`RE-F-01`),
   5,018/5,018 Wwise clips decode (`RE-M`), 1,629/1,637 typed materials resolve
   (`RE-C-01`), and 99.92 % of 134,768 placed mesh instances resolve to an exported
   GLB (`RE-B`). The pipeline is not the weak link.
2. **Consumption is far thinner than the gates suggest.** 81 of 144 generated tables
   have no consumer anywhere (`RE-A-03`), 92 of 995 clips are bound (`RE-F-09`),
   45 of 1,296 audio events are reachable (`RE-M-08`), 159+1 of 976 Guildhall light
   records become nodes (`RE-D-02`), and 8 of 54 hearing stim rows have an emitter
   (`RE-I-05`). Extraction without ingestion is the defining property of this port.
3. **The shortfall is structured, not random.** It concentrates where a *producer*
   (dump→table) or a *behavioural consumer* (table→per-frame) is missing. Where both
   exist — loot rows, door geometry, run clock, patrol points, device action graphs —
   fidelity is 1:1 or near it.
4. **Three defects destroy player state**, and none is visual: a corrupt save is
   silently replaced by a fresh profile (`RE-N-03`), a co-op client fails a run the
   host completed (`RE-N-11`), and the pending-payout outbox has no production caller
   (`RE-N-06`).
5. **Two policies explain most of the interior look, and they are not the same one.**
   The Guildhall's darkness is light eviction — a 160-light budget on a 976-record map,
   cutting at an authored energy of 25.0 so every candle dies (`RE-D-02`,
   `C1-01`). The Den, which keeps its hand-placed fills and *matches* the reference
   (p50 17 vs 18), instead shows the GI/IBL deficit: no Lumen, no SSIL/SDFGI, and an
   authored SkyLight cubemap that is never loaded (`RE-D-12/-14`, `C1-03`). The
   documented "43.5 vs 52.6" therefore measures the Den's GI gap, not exposure, and it
   does not transfer to the Guildhall, where fixing GI alone would move ~10 %.
6. **Audio is the most complete extraction and the least consumed system.** The control
   plane (130 buses, 1,978 attenuations, 242 switch containers, 203 music segments, 98
   emitters) reaches zero consumers (`RE-M-01/-02/-04/-05/-07`); the port ships six
   invented buses and one 60 m model.
7. **The gates are green because they measure presence.** 99.6 % is package-stem
   coverage (12,657/12,703), `--refresh-stamps` accepts the drift it detects, the
   `.usmap` is unpinned, and at least five suites are tautological or self-fulfilling
   (S2-12, 90.1/90.2, 80.3/80.4, T2-15-A, 55c) — §4.2.
8. **Documentation drift is a first-class defect, not housekeeping.** At least 21
   tracked statements in `AGENTS.md`, `README.md`, `docs/FIDELITY_GAPS.md`,
   `docs/REFERENCE_BASELINE.md` and `docs/PERFORMANCE.md` contradict the data, several
   in the direction that makes the port look more faithful than it is — §4.4.
9. **Substitution is repeatedly presented as parity.** Six invented audio buses for
   130, a six-state FSM for 21 state trees, a 1 m melee for a 20 m ranged curve, a
   hand-drawn `[TAB]` map for 13 `TATWorldMapBoundary` actors, lock levels from
   `spawn_index % n`, an invented result panel and an invented sensitivity ladder —
   §4.3, §6.
10. **The honest ceiling is a 3.5–17 % consumption ratio** across every
    high-cardinality family, and 0 % for five families with no consumer. Four producers,
    two consumers and one gate change would move more fidelity than any renderer tuning
    — §4.5, §7.

**What the chain table proves.** Both ends are strong and the middle is not: the
extractor loses little it is asked to read, the runtime renders what it is given, and
the loss is in the two joins — dump→table (missing producer) and table→behaviour
(missing consumer). "Not extracted" in a lane almost always means "no producer reads
the dump", not "the dump lacks the data".

**Producer-count drift is itself evidence.** Four counts in four tracked places means
nobody re-counted when the pipeline grew — the same failure mode behind the brief's
21-producer claim. §7's gate change is aimed at it.

### A — Pipeline & data integrity

The extractor's six typed readers cover the heavy assets; the generic dump covers the
rest at 99.1 % package coverage. The producers are the bottleneck: 19 `parse_*.py`
files produce 144 tables, but 81 have no reader (`RE-A-03`), including the StateTree
payload (784 KB), `vfx/systems.json` (1.37 MB, tests only) and `videos.json`. Five
runtime directories are literals in `game_data.gd:106-111` with no producer behind
them, so the curves they expect are constants. The gate never parses GDScript:
`rg -c "game/src|.gd" tools/manifest_audit.py` → 0.

### B — World / levels

The strongest domain. Every shipped `.umap` normalises and every referenced mesh path
resolves, the 6 residual paths being `/Engine` primitives served from `Raw/` (`RE-B`
§1b). LevelInstance merging is correct (138 instances / 32,166 meshes) but scoped to
`/Game/Maps/`, dropping 52 actors / ~2,400 meshes uncounted (`RE-B-02`). The largest
loss is `SplineMeshComponent.SplineParams`: 489 spline pieces collapse to their actor
root and 1,080 duplicates are then deleted (`RE-B-04`) — truncated walls, railings,
track. FTUE also loads `FTUE_Building_JewelryStore` twice, 1,693 meshes (`RE-B-03`).

### C — Materials & textures

1,629 of 1,637 typed material assets are written and every shipped level resolves a
material (`RE-C-01`). The loss is in *which* texture and *what colour*: 307 albedos are
guessed because 315 master slots use non-standard names (`MainTexture`, `EmisTexture`,
`RuneTexture` …), and 541 authored **linear** tints go to Godot's sRGB `albedo_color`
at `level_loader.gd:1481` — 110,729 binds, 39 % of the map (`RE-C-05`, `RE-C-08`).
`BasePropertyOverrides` is read for `TwoSided` only, so 15 materials blend wrongly and
5 stained-glass panes are opaque (`RE-C-03`); 65 meshes / 3,089 instances still show
the WorldGrid checker (`RE-C-09`).

### D — Lighting / sky / weather

`DESKTOP_MAX_LIGHTS = 160` (`mobile_profile.gd:14`, applied `level_loader.gd:5395`)
gives Guildhall 159 generic + 1 rect light from 976 extracted records (`RE-D-02`); the
976→915 dedupe is not reproducible as written (§8). Intensity is an invented tanh
anchored at the authored median (`LIGHT_MEDIAN_INTENSITY 15.0`,
`LIGHT_ENERGY_SOFT_CEIL 3.5`, `level_loader.gd:234-288`) while three docs still say
`1/15` linear (`RE-D-05`). GI is SSIL only against a Lumen build; SkyLight and
SkyAtmosphere ship on zero playable maps and the sky is an invented gradient
(`RE-D-12`, `RE-D-15`). Fog density is authored × 0.03 with height falloff unread
(`weather_profile.gd:74,663`).

### E — VFX / Niagara

Inventory is exact: 120 `NS_` systems + 2 `NE_` emitters extracted, 3,695 placements,
3,303 unique (`RE-E-01`). Runtime is a stand-in: one additive untextured billboard
MultiMesh per system, tint and size chosen by name regex, catalogue unread
(`RE-E-02`). 2,260 of 3,308 live placements (68 %) are event-gated in the build and
permanently lit in the port, so destroyed and deactivated device states draw at once
(`RE-E-03`). 1,044 per-placement parameter blobs decode from the JSON and are never
read (`RE-E-04`). The four "REFERENCE_MATCHED" set-piece builders have no gameplay
caller (`RE-E-08`), and the physical footprint decal is a UI icon (`RE-E-11`).

### F — Animation

All 995 keyframed clips convert 1:1 (`RE-F-01`); 92 names are wired and 85 reachable,
i.e. 9.2 %, leaving 903 orphans (`RE-F-09`). The 3,594 authored notifies (1,313
footsteps) are extracted, exposed as accessors and consumed by nobody (`RE-F-06`), which
is why melee damage lands at swing frame 0 instead of the authored 0.92–1.17 s window
(`RE-F-12`). Montage sections and slots are ignored, so upper-body attacks stop the
legs (`RE-F-07`). The 3P thief body is hidden and has no locomotion animation at all
(`RE-F-10`), and the lantern guard's run clip is 5.39 m/s against a 7.6 constant, so
its playback clamps to 0.35 and the feet slide at ~1.9 m/s (`RE-F-13`).

### G — Physics / traversal

Every collider is synthesised from the render mesh: 8,382 authored `AggGeom` primitives
(5,922 convex, 2,334 box, 81 capsule, 45 sphere) are in the dump and read by nothing
(`RE-G-01`, `RE-Q-05`); 24,310 runtime shapes cover 62,950 instances = 0.386 per
instance (`RE-G-02`). There is no navmesh — 3 bounds volumes, 16 modifiers, 126 nav
links and 1,707 guard posts are unconsumed and `NavigationServer3D` has one unreachable
call site (`RE-G-15`, `RE-O-10`). Movement constants are hand-mirrored, not read:
`character_movement.json` has no consumer and the capsule has already drifted 0.91 vs
0.89 (`RE-G-05`). Acceleration is an invented `steer = 14` lerp
(`thief_character.gd:1323`), and guards fall at 18.0 m/s² (`guard_ai.gd:223`).

### H — Input

The authored layer is entirely unread: 65 `IA_*` and 5 `IMC_*` assets dumped, zero
normalised, and `game_data.gd:109` points at a directory that does not exist
(`RE-A-04`, `RE-H-01`). The port hand-writes 21 actions against 52 authored, five keys
contradicting the build (C, E, R, V, G) (`RE-H-02`). Gamepad look and fire do not exist
— six joypad events in the project file (`RE-H-03/-04`) — and rebinding, shipped with 17
cells and conflict text, is absent (`RE-H-05`). Sensitivity is a free
0.0005–0.008 rad/px slider (`settings_ui.gd:24-25,189`) where the build authors a
five-step ladder per device (`RE-H-08`). Glyphs are parsed out of prose, not queried
from bindings (`RE-H-06`).

### I — AI

Five controllers, 21 state trees / 459 states / 467 transitions and 12 EQS queries
ship; the port runs one six-state FSM plus a wander loop (`RE-I-01`; 0 of 13 top-level
guard branches 1:1, `RE-I-08`). Sight range and cone are 1:1 but five fields are
dropped and the close-range bypass is 3.5 m against a 1.0 m authored minimum
(`RE-I-02`). Hearing holds 54 rows and 22 surfaces exactly, but 8 rows have an emitter
and walls do not attenuate (`RE-I-05`). Seven spawn buckets are unparsed, so
`KeyHolder` and `Hauntstable` never spawn (`RE-I-07`). Combat is a 1 m melee where the
build authors a 20 m curve (10 → 2), accuracy stacks and a shock debuff (`RE-I-11`).

### J — Missions / progression

Eleven mission rows load, 6 are startable, 3 are `Example.*` templates and remain
selectable (`RE-J-01`). Quest-graph *modules* are never walked, so 21 clue facts and
every vault objective are unreachable (`RE-J-03`). The 16-contract chain is dropped and
every contract is available from a new save (`RE-J-07`). XP is the sharpest invented
value: the curve is 1:1 but the award is `max(100, score/2)` (`save_system.gd:921`)
while 11 authored `XP.*` categories sit unread and the cap is 200 against an authored
25 (`RE-J-10`). The endgame ladder is flat 1200 s at every difficulty against an
authored 1200/600/300 (`RE-J-13`, `F1b C-2`).

### K — Gameplay systems

Loot values, names, tags and bucket distribution are 1:1 (99 rows, 20 actors, 44 quest
actors) and the device action/power graph is exact: 6,005 + 1,475 edges, 0 unresolved
(`RE-K-14`). The losses are behavioural: authored large-carry multipliers ×0.6 walk /
×0.3 climb are plumbed and unused (`RE-K-04`), 16 loot containers are dropped by a
five-class allowlist (`RE-K-06`), steal time up to 4 s is ignored (`RE-K-05`), and
container locks are invented from `spawn_index % n` (`loot_container.gd:186-193`,
`RE-K-07`). 107 Guildhall devices are inert, including all six vault doors (`RE-K-10`).
Run clock, escape markers, payout idempotency and the gadget numbers are 1:1
(`RE-K-23/-24/-27`).

### L — UI / localisation

The widget inventory is 1:1 — 225 WBPs, 327 dumps, 617 text refs, no silent drops —
and the runtime rebuilds ~13 % of the art: 37 of 279 textures, 9 screens of ~30
(`RE-L-01`). Three screen families are absent: post-match + XP + unlocks (12 WBPs, 71
strings), progression/contract-complete/quest notes (~8), tutorial (25)
(`RE-L-09/-10/-27`); the result panel at `hud.gd:1932-1961` is invented where the build
ships `ST_PostMatch`. Localisation is a façade: 54 StringTables / 1,687 entries, one
read, 17 labels wired (`RE-L-15`). No authored 9-slice frame is used and type runs at
~50 % of the authored size (`RE-L-19/-20`).

### M — Audio

Media extraction is genuinely 1:1 (5,018/5,018 clips; 5,862/5,868 links). Everything
above the clip is a substitute: 6 port buses (Master + Music/SFX/Ambience/UI/Dialogue)
with zero `AudioEffect` against 130 authored buses and 76 effects (`RE-M-01`); one 60 m
inverse model against 1,978 sharesets (`RE-M-02`); 6 hub-hardcoded ambience keys
against 98 placed emitters (`RE-M-04`); no music against 21 play events and 203
segments (`RE-M-05`). Reachability is 45/1,296 = 3.5 %; `play_sfx` and `fire_event`
have no callers, so guards, tools, doors, lockpicks and 686 VO lines are silent
(`RE-M-08`, `RE-M-10`). Surface audio is seven substring keys against 28+14 authored
switch mappings (`RE-M-09`).

### N — State / save / co-op

The run model is port-local and partly duplicated (44 fields, 3 dead, 2 lossy mirrors,
`RE-N-01`). The save is temp+rename but not fsynced, and a parse failure falls through
`if parsed is Dictionary:` with no else, so a corrupt file becomes `_defaults()` and is
rewritten (`RE-N-03`); `save_schema = 8` is a label, not a history (`RE-N-02`). The
outbox cannot double-bank but has no production caller, so a failed write loses a
payout while `bank_payout` reports success (`RE-N-06`). Replication covers pose and
doors only; a successful team escape fails the client and banks a stash-only timeout
(`RE-N-11`). The Den hub can fail itself on a 5400 s clock (`RE-N-15`).

### O — Map logic & flow

107/107 levels normalise, but only 57 are reachable from the UI, 25 via LevelInstance
merge and **25 never load** (`RE-O` summary). The quest-graph module layer — security
composition, vault choice, weather blockers — is extracted (10 nodes) and executed by
nobody, so every deploy spawns the union of all variants (`RE-O-04`).
`OldManor_PathBlockers`, an always-loaded sublevel with 156 collidable meshes, is
missing from the hardcoded list (`RE-O-03`). Scene variants are selected 104 times and
applied 0 (`RE-O-05`). The 13 `TATWorldMapBoundary` actors are unextracted; `[TAB]` is
a schematic (`RE-O-10`).

### P — Placement

Transforms are exact: 8/8 spot checks, 1,692/1,739 bulk marker matches (`RE-P-01`).
Loot and civilians are floor-exact, but 183 pickups still take a blanket +0.35 m offset
(`RE-P-07`). 62,953 of 63,578 static meshes spawn (99.0 %); the losses are silent caps —
274 dedupe, 243 name-skip, 41 wardbox, 160/915 lights, 256 decals/map (`RE-P-16`). 3,290
Guildhall instances carry a reflected basis with no winding fix (`RE-P-08`). The prep
skyline is labelled expansion but 2–5× oversized for its contract (`RE-P-04`); the
clocktower and vista are unlabelled (`RE-P-13/-15`).

### Q — Meshing

The writer is faithful where it writes: 2,065 GLB, 4,193 primitives, LOD0 with 8 UV
sets, vertex colours, 4 bone weights, slot order preserved (`RE-Q-01`, `RE-Q-12`). It
does not write LOD chains (127 SM / 128 SK), nanite density (1,657 meshes; 649.6 M
authored vs 71.1 M drawn triangles in Guildhall) or authored collision
(`RE-Q-02/-03/-05`). The loader then overrides correct slot order with a name-substring
match, mis-binding 55 meshes / 776 instances (`RE-Q-06`); 1,536 instances render the
placeholder grid (`RE-Q-07`) and 209 MB of 542 MB is empty UV payload (`RE-Q-11`).

## 4. The five systemic patterns

### 4.1 Extraction without ingestion

The pipeline dumps UE objects at near-total coverage and then declines to read them.
This is not "hard to extract"; it is "no producer was written", a scheduling decision.

| Dumped, unread | Count | Consequence | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `WidgetTree` (WidgetBlueprint) | **225** | UI rebuilt by hand; ~13 % of widget art reused | `RE-A-04`, `RE-L-01` |
| `InputAction` / `InputMappingContext` | **65 / 5** | 21 hand-authored actions, five keys wrong, no rebinding | `RE-A-04`, `RE-H-01/-02` |
| `StateTree` | **21** trees / 459 states / 467 transitions | one six-state FSM; 0/13 branches 1:1 | `RE-A-02`, `RE-I-01/-08` |
| Niagara packages | **122** (120 `NS_` + 2 `NE_`) | one untextured billboard per system; 1,044 parameter blobs ignored | `RE-E-01/-02/-04` |
| `AggGeom` collision primitives | **8,382** | every collider synthesised from art; 0.386 shapes/instance | `RE-G-01`, `RE-Q-05` |
| `CurveFloat` / `Vector` / `LinearColor` | **76 / 6 / 5** | alertness ramp is a step; gamepad and carry curves absent | `RE-A-01` |
| `DefaultGame.ini` | whole file | difficulty loot multiplier, endgame ladder, XP amounts, FTUE bankroll, perks all unread | `F1b C-2/-4`, `RE-J-10/-12/-13`, `RE-K-02/-03` |
| Wwise control plane | 130 buses, 1,978 attenuations, 242 switch containers, 203 segments, 98 emitters | audio is 6 invented buses and 45 reachable events | `RE-M-01…-07` |

Two properties make this worse than a backlog.

First, **the unread data is often the *behaviour* half of a system whose *values* are
already 1:1.** The run clock reads the authored mission row correctly (`RE-K-23`), but
`MatchMainPhaseDuration 1320` and `EndgameDurationsByDifficulty 1200/600/300` sit
unread beside it, so the clock's difficulty dimension is invented. Patrol paths are
exact (210/673) and unusable without a navmesh (`RE-I-06`, `RE-G-15`); hearing rows are
exact (54/54) and 46 have no emitter (`RE-I-05`). The missing piece is a consumer.

Second, **the pipeline's own validation is outside the gate.** `check_logic_data.py`
validates `guard_behavior.json` and no gate runs it (`RE-A-02`); `parse_vfx.py` writes
a 1.37 MB catalogue only tests read. A producer can be correct, unread and green at
once — the state the audit found 81 times.

### 4.2 Gates that measure presence, not behaviour

`make audit` is a strong *content-presence* gate and a weak *fidelity* gate: mount
parity (12,703 paths), disk presence (12,657/12,703 = 99.6 %), 13 count assertions,
`errors.log` honesty and per-producer drift pinning (`RE-A` §3). None reads a field
value; none parses GDScript.

Four specific weaknesses compound:

- **Coverage is a stem test.** `_stem()` looks for a file of that name anywhere under
  `extracted/`, so a renamed export passes (`SK_Tarp*` → `SKM_Tarp*`, `RE-A-08`) and a
  dumped-but-empty JSON passes. The 46 `disk_missing` entries are labelled
  "movies/shader/editor-only", but 38 are content-bearing: 18 movie wrappers, 13 graybox
  templates, 4 geometry collections, 3 gameplay assets (`RE-A` §3).
- **`--refresh-stamps` accepts the drift it detects.** The refresh path requires only
  that the file count did not fall and the hash changed (`manifest_audit.py:1279-1311`),
  so a silently truncated field is re-baselined by the same command that is supposed to
  catch it (`RE-A-09`).
- **The `.usmap` and the built extractor are unpinned.** The baseline hashes the source
  files and the native `.so`, but not the mapping file that determines every property
  name, nor `bin/Release/net10.0/tat-extract` (`RE-A-10`). A different `.usmap` would
  rewrite the tree and the audit would call it drift, not a different input.
- **Several suites assert the implementation, not the behaviour.** `T2-15-A` passes if
  the HUD text contains `"2.0"` — which the embedded shader's `UV * 2.0 - 1.0` already
  guarantees (`C4-gameplay-code.md:414-420`). `90.1`/`90.2` compare
  `save_schema` against `SAVE_SCHEMA_VERSION`, the same constant, so the failure message
  `"!= 8"` cannot catch a bump (`RE-N` §tests). `80.3`/`80.4` grep source text for a
  call site (`RE-N` §tests). `S2-12` compares `last_payout.free_play` with
  `GameState.free_play` — the flag with itself (`RE-N-18`). `55c` freezes physics and
  calls `_try_blackjack` directly, so no AI tick and no hold race runs (`RE-I-14`). The
  VFX guard even *forbids the fix*: naming `VectorVM` or dropping "ParameterData … not
  decoded" fails T2-06-A / T4-12 / suite 52 (`RE-E-05`).

The gates are not useless — they caught real W7 regressions — but "all gates green"

## 7. What "faithful" would require

Not a rewrite. Four producers, two consumers and one gate change would move the
consumption ratios more than any renderer or gameplay work, because each one sits under
several findings at once.

**Producer 1 — `DefaultGame.ini`.** One file, six settings blocks, and it unblocks the
difficulty loot multiplier (`1/2/3`), the endgame ladder (`1200/600/300`), 11 `XP.*`
amounts and `MaximumLevel=25`, the FTUE bankroll, the scoring bonuses,
`DifficultyToVigilantLoopCount` (`2/4/8`) and `DefaultLootSlotSize`. It converts six
invented values into authored ones at once (`F1b C-2/-4`, `RE-J-10/-12/-13`,
`RE-K-02/-03`).

**Producer 2 — Enhanced Input.** `parse_input.py` over
`extracted/json/Gameplay/Input/**`, `DefaultInput.ini` and the ini ladder →
`bindings.json` + `settings_model.json`: 52 actions instead of 21, the correct five
keys, the two response curves, the sensitivity ladder, the deadzones and a glyph
source — the prerequisite for rebinding (`RE-H-01/-05/-08/-10`).

**Producer 3 — `AggGeom` collision.** A pass over `BodySetup.AggGeom` (8,382 prims,
`CollisionTraceFlag`, `NoCollision`/`QueryOnly`) → `mesh_collision.json`, consumed by
`_get_mesh_and_shape()` with the heuristics as a counted fallback. No re-extraction:
the data is in the dump. Removes the name-token heuristics and most "invisible wall /
sealed doorway" cases (`RE-G-01/-03`, `RE-Q-05`).

**Producer 4 — the Wwise control plane.** `parse_audio.py` over the bank hierarchy: 130
buses with props and FX sharesets, 1,978 attenuations and 17 per-event radii, 242 switch
containers, 203 music segments/tracks, 311+302 reverb components, 98 emitters, and
`MaxAttenuationRadius` re-read into `audio_events.json`. Media is already 1:1; this is
the missing half (`RE-M-01/-02/-04/-05/-07`).

**Consumer 1 — StateTrees.** Either flatten the 21 trees / 467 transitions into the FSM
(the cheap path) or run a small BT/utility interpreter over the extracted graph. The
data is 784 KB of JSON with a validator already written; no gate runs it. This is also
where reaction roles, the two-investigator cap and vision stimuli land (`RE-A-02`,
`RE-I-01/-08/-12`).

**Consumer 2 — Niagara parameters.** Decode `OverrideParameters.ParameterData` via
`SortedParameterOffsets` (1,044 blobs), resample the 120 curve LUTs, and read
`bAutoActivate` per placement. That fixes flame/rain/beam colour, 68 % of activation
states and the rain rate the docs call unrecoverable — without touching the VectorVM
interpreter, which remains the one genuine research project (`RE-E-03/-04/-06`).

**The gate change.** `manifest_audit.py` should fail when a declared producer output has
no reader in `game/src/`, with a short allowlist for build manifests
(`audio_library.json`, `mesh_materials_provenance.json`, `devices.json`). Today
`rg -c "game/src|.gd" tools/manifest_audit.py` = 0. A consumer check would have failed
on 81 tables the day they were added and would have caught the StateTree payload, the
4 MB of analysis JSON and the empty curve directories — the only mechanism here that
stops the *next* 81-table drift. Pair it with a `.usmap` + built-binary hash and one
content assertion per geometry family (`levels_with_meshes`, `collision_shapes`,
`light_count`) so `RE-B-12`'s path-based checks stop standing in for geometry
(`RE-A` §3, `RE-A-09/-10`).

More captures, pixel comparisons and shader tuning would not help: the frames
already show dark interiors and a violet ward. The work is ingestion.

**The two real ceilings**, for the record: cooked material graphs are not decompilable
(so `M_Env*` roughness semantics remain an inference, `RE-C-07`), and VectorVM opcode
semantics are not in the data (so arbitrary particle logic cannot be replayed,
`RE-E-06`). Everything else that the docs call a hard limit — splines, 1P idle,
particle rates, decals — is a parser or a caller that was never written.

## 8. Method and limits

**What was measured vs read.** The lanes measured with `rg`, `python3`, `magick`,
`ffmpeg` and `git`; they read the runtime source. This synthesis measured nothing new:
it re-read the cited constants at their lines (light budget, fog scale, exposures,
sensitivity range, clip rates, guard gravity, scene grade) and took every count from a
lane finding. No Godot run, no producer, no `make normalize`, no audit refresh and no
git mutation was performed, per the audit brief.

**Read-only constraint.** The repo, `extracted/` and `game/data/` stayed read-only,
which bounds what could be *proved*: claims about what a frame looks like or a collider
feels like are either a lane's committed measurement or `not verified`. The strongest
claims here are about data and code, and they are the ones with commands attached.

exactly and one did not:
