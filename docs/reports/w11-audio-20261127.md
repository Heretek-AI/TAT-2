## 4. W11-C — `FS_Layers`

`layer_for()` produces five of the ten authored values: `Puddle` (Puddle,
GroundWet), `Glass_Broken` (BrokenGlass), `Foliage`, `Grass`, `Nothing`
(everything else). The other five are not produced:

- `MetalPipe`, `MetalSheet`, `Glass` are shared `FS_Material` families the
  material switch already selects, so re-selecting them as a layer would ban
  the same clips twice (`Metal` is layer-only; the port's `metal` token maps to
  `MetalThick`/`MetalGrate` and leaves `Metal` at `Nothing`).
- `Slime` is both an `FS_Material` value and a layer value, but it is an
  authored tool effect (`Eqp_SlimeTool_*`) with no world-surface record to
  derive it from.
- The `FS_Layers` group has no single default: 4 of its 6 containers default
  `Nothing` and 2 default `Foliage`. The port explicitly sets `Nothing` (an
  authored value) for every surface it cannot justify.

Measured on `Play_FS_Player_Walk` (319 media):

| Selection | Media |
|---|---:|
| `FS_Material=Stone`, `FS_Layers=Nothing` | **44** |
| `FS_Material=Stone`, `FS_Layers=Puddle` | **52** (+8 layer-only recordings) |
| `FS_Material=WoodFloor` | 44 (the bank's WoodBeam family) |

The player passes the authored surface name (`SurfaceResolver.layer_for(
footstep_surface())`) because the `earth` pool token collapses
Grass/Foliage/Dirt; a token-only caller still gets Wet→Puddle and
Glass→Nothing.

## 5. W11-D — RTPC callers, unit domains and exact values

Every caller converts its game-side unit into the *authored game-parameter
domain* before `set_rtpc`; the authored curve is never rescaled.

| RTPC | Game-side unit | Authored domain | Conversion |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Character_Stamina_RTPC` | player stamina `0..max_stamina` (260) | `0..100` | `stamina / max_stamina * 100` |
| `Character_FS_isCrouching` | bool | `0..1` | `1.0`/`0.0` |
| `Character_Fall_Velocity_Z` | `velocity.y` m/s | `-5000..500` cm/s | `* 100`, clamped |
| `Weather_Rain_Intensity` | preset `rain.intensity` | `0..1` | clamp |
| `Character_DisguiseIntegrity` | player integrity `0..100` | `0..1` | `integrity / 100`, clamped |

| RTPC | Caller | Authored curve (exact) |
|---|---|---|
| `Character_Stamina_RTPC` | player, 10 Hz | `[0,0] [63.67521,-0.317507] [100,-1.0]` |
| `Character_FS_isCrouching` | player, 0/1 on change; gain applied to footstep playback | `ActorMixer:492723062` `0→0`, `1→-0.608844` dB |
| `Character_Fall_Velocity_Z` | player, 10 Hz | `LayerContainer:350464786` `-5000→+0.498813`, `-500→-1.0` dB; pitch `-100` cents at -5000 |
| `Weather_Rain_Intensity` | mission weather preset | `0→-1.0`, `0.65→0.0` dB |
| `Character_DisguiseIntegrity` | `AudioManager` on `disguise_changed` | `[0,0] [0.39834,-1] [1,-1]`; `0→0`, `0.25→-0.627605`, `1→-1.0` dB |

The five curves target `ActorMixer`, `LayerContainer` and
`RandomSequenceContainer` nodes the port does not instantiate as buses; they are
recorded and exposed in `mix_state().rtpc` rather than applied to a global bus,
which would fight the slider + duck contract of `test_audio_mix.gd`. The crouch
curve is the one audible binding (footstep playback). The `blood_moon` preset
authors `rain.intensity = 10.0`; the caller clamps to the RTPC domain 0..1
(documented, not silently rescaled).

### 5.1 Fixes after verification (`1e63c79` → this commit)

1. **Disguise value domain.** `_on_disguise_changed` passed the player's
   `0..100` integrity straight into the authored `0..1` curve, so every value
   `>= 1` evaluated at the 1.0 endpoint. The new
   `push_disguise_integrity()` normalises `integrity / 100` (clamped 0..1)
   before `set_rtpc`; the curve keeps its authored points. Suite 121 pins
   `0/25/50/75/100 → 0/0.25/0.5/0.75/1.0` and `25 → -0.627605 dB`
   (mid-knee, not the endpoint), through both the helper and the signal
   handler.
2. **Disguise signal lifetime.** `_vo_player_bound` was a one-shot bool, so a
   new `ThiefCharacter` (one per contract) never got its `disguise_changed`
   connected. `_sync_vo_sources` now tracks `_vo_bound_player`: it clears a
   freed binding, disconnects a replaced player, connects the current one
   exactly once, and resets the director's cooldowns on a player change.
   Suite 122 drives two fake players through the real `_process` path and pins
   one connection each, the old connection dropped, the second player's RTPC
   (`25 → 0.25`), the `disguise_ending` line/subtitle, and the cleared binding
   at teardown.
